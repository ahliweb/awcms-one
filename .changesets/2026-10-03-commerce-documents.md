# Held sales, quotations, work orders, and numbered receipts/invoices (issue #286, ADR-0029)

The sale had no documents around it: no way to park a cart, quote a price, track a service job, or print a numbered receipt or invoice, and the OSPOS way of getting those (one overloaded sale row) was ruled out. They now exist as **separate records** around the unchanged order, with seven tables (`awcms_commerce_document_sequences`, `…_held_sales`, `…_quotations`, `…_quotation_versions`, `…_work_orders`, `…_work_order_events`, `…_documents` — migrations `980`–`982` under `apps/cms/sql/`, starting at `apps/cms/sql/980_awcms_commerce_documents_schema.sql`). The "why", the invoice-versus-accounts-receivable boundary and the options weighed are in [ADR-0029](../docs/adr/0029-commerce-documents-are-separate-records-and-numbered-documents-are-immutable-order-snapshots.md).

- New: park a POS cart and resume it (lines only — no price, **no stock reserved**, owned by the cashier, expires after 24 hours by default, single-use resume); create a priced, versioned quotation with a validity period, accept it (which pins the version shown) and convert it into an order through the ordinary POS path (created at today's price with the balance due; a changed total is refused unless accepted; exactly one order per quotation, race-safe and idempotent); run work orders through a status machine with an append-only history, optionally from an accepted quotation; issue a numbered, **immutable** receipt or invoice for a finalized order (`INV-2026-000042` — gapless per tenant, type and UTC year, allocated in the same transaction as the row) and render it as JSON, a 42-column plain-text receipt, or a print-ready, script-free HTML page under a locked-down CSP. All mutations take an `Idempotency-Key`; events `awcms.commerce.quotation.{accepted,converted}`, `awcms.commerce.work_order.status_changed`, `awcms.commerce.document.issued`; admin screen `/admin/commerce-documents` and hold/resume controls on `/admin/commerce-pos`.
- No second monetary authority: a document's money is a copy a database trigger verifies against the order, and a quotation never writes an order itself. A commercial document is **not** an accounts-receivable invoice — that stays a future, separate record that will reference a document.
- Thirteen new permissions, none implied by `commerce.pos.create` or by each other: `commerce.held_sales.{read,create,update,approve}`, `commerce.quotations.{read,create,update}`, `commerce.quotation_conversions.create` (a conversion also needs `commerce.pos_due.create`), `commerce.work_orders.{read,create,update}`, `commerce.documents.{read,create}`. Existing tenants do not gain them retroactively.
- **Backward compatible, opt-in.** The whole surface is behind the new `documents` feature flag (commerce settings → Features), which defaults OFF: with it off every new route answers `409 FEATURE_DISABLED`, the sidebar entry is hidden, and checkout, POS and the order APIs behave exactly as before (no existing table gained a column).
- Hardening: `awcms_app` loses `DELETE` on all seven tables (and `UPDATE` on versions, events and documents); composite tenant-safe FKs; guard triggers freeze identity/provenance and allow only legal status moves; `security-readiness.ts` asserts the exact privilege sets.
- Not here yet (ADR-0029 Deferred): an accounts-receivable invoice and credit notes, voiding or replacing a document, a receipt per payment, e-mail/WhatsApp/PDF delivery, a tenant-timezone numbering year, stock reservation by a quotation.
