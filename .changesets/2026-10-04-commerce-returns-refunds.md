# Returns, refunds and exchanges with inventory and payment compensation (issue #287, ADR-0033)

Commerce could record that money went back and could cancel a whole order, but had no RETURN: nothing tied "two of five units came back" to stock, money, loyalty points, affiliate commission and the sales reports. It does now, as new rows that compensate through the ledgers that already exist: four tables (`awcms_commerce_returns`, `…_return_lines`, `…_refunds`, `…_refund_compensations` — migrations `994`–`997` under `apps/cms/sql/`, starting at `apps/cms/sql/994_awcms_commerce_returns_schema.sql`). The "why", the options weighed (incl. the inventory boundary), the threat model and the deferrals are in [ADR-0033](../docs/adr/0033-returns-refunds-and-exchanges-are-additive-records-that-compensate-through-the-existing-ledgers.md).

- New: staff record a return or exchange from the order detail ("Returns and refunds" panel and a three-step wizard): units per order line with a mandatory reason and a stock disposition (`restock` puts units back into sellable stock; `damaged` and `quarantine` are recorded and move none), bounded by what remains eligible — under the order-row lock and by a database trigger, so two concurrent returns of the last unit cannot both succeed. Value is exact to the cent (several partial returns of a line add up to the line). The refund is planned along the ORIGINAL payments newest first, each capped at what it can still give back (a trigger caps refund legs and, new here, payment-ledger reversals), and every leg that can settle now is settled in the same transaction: cash (optionally out of the caller's open register), a manual QRIS/bank refund, value back onto a gift card, or store credit (a new account's code is shown once and never stored). Nothing finalised is edited — no order, order line or payment row is ever updated.
- Gateway refunds call the provider with NO transaction open, keyed by the refund leg's id so a retry can never refund twice (`PaymentGatewayProvider.refund` is an optional port method; the log adapter and Midtrans implement it). A leg the provider cannot make is settled offline under the new high-risk `commerce.refunds_offline.approve` with a stated reason.
- Compensations are proportional and idempotent: loyalty points reversed (`reversal:refund:{id}`), affiliate commission given back (`adjusted_amount`; a paid commission becomes a clawback), and the sales-report projections net the return on the stream they already read (live equals rebuild; a cancel after a partial return nets to zero).
- Five new permissions, existing verbs only: `commerce.returns.{read,create}`, `commerce.refunds.{read,create}`, `commerce.refunds_offline.approve`; a refund also needs `commerce.payments.revoke`. Existing tenants do not gain them retroactively. New endpoints under `/api/v1/commerce/orders/{id}/returns` and `/api/v1/commerce/returns*`; events `awcms.commerce.return.recorded` and `awcms.commerce.refund.settled`.
- **Backward compatible, opt-in.** The whole surface is behind the new `returns` feature flag, which defaults OFF: with it off every owner route answers `409 FEATURE_DISABLED` and the order detail shows no panel. Heads-up for reviewers: `sql/995` narrows the loyalty ledger's "one reversal per earn" unique index to non-refund reversals and adds `source_type = 'refund'`; `awcms_commerce_order_events` gains a nullable `return_id`; `awcms_commerce_affiliate_commissions` gains `adjusted_amount`.
- Not here yet (ADR-0033 Deferred): multi-location stock movements for damaged/quarantine units (#282), automatic tax/insurance refund, customer-initiated return requests, a scheduled refund job, voiding an open return, a returns report.
