# Gift cards and store credit as a closed-loop liability ledger (issue #288, ADR-0029)

Commerce had no way to sell value ahead of goods. It does now, as a LIABILITY ledger rather than a mutable balance field or a discount: three tables (`awcms_commerce_stored_value_programs`, `…_accounts`, `…_ledger` — migrations `980`–`983` under `apps/cms/sql/`, starting at `apps/cms/sql/980_awcms_commerce_stored_value_schema.sql`) hold the per-tenant configuration, the cards/credits and the append-only signed entries (`issue`, `load`, `redeem`, `refund`, `adjust`, `expire`, `disable`, `enable`). The "why", the threat model, the privacy and the regulatory boundary are in [ADR-0029](../docs/adr/0029-stored-value-is-a-closed-loop-liability-ledger.md).

- New: staff configure a program per kind, ISSUE a card or credit (the code is shown once, in the issuing response, and printed on a receipt from it — it is never stored), load value onto it, adjust it with a reason, disable/enable it, and run an expiry sweep and a consistency check; a liability report (`GET /api/v1/reports/commerce/stored-value`) shows issued, loaded, redeemed, refunded, adjusted (up and down kept apart), expired and what is outstanding. Admin screen `/admin/commerce-stored-value`; a "Gift cards and store credit" toggle in the commerce Features section; event `awcms.commerce.stored_value.entry_recorded`.
- Redemption is a TENDER on the payment ledger (ADR-0025): `gift_card` and `store_credit` joined the tender types, POS `tenders[]` and `POST …/orders/{id}/payments` accept them with a `storedValueCode`, and the payment leg and its mirror ledger entry are written in one transaction (a database trigger and a deferred constraint trigger refuse either half alone). Change never applies to a card; a card that is unknown, disabled, expired or too poor refuses the whole sale before any row is written; concurrent redemptions of one card can never overdraw (the database refuses it, whatever the application does). A reversal of such a payment credits the SAME account back, or is `409 PAYMENT_NOT_REVERSIBLE` when the program disallows it — there is no cash-out and no transfer in this design.
- The code is 100 bits from the platform CSPRNG (an unambiguous 32-symbol alphabet plus a check character); only a tenant-scoped sha256 and its last four characters are stored; it appears in no log, audit attribute, event payload, idempotency row or response after issue (an integration test reads every table as text to prove it). Failed lookups are one neutral `404 STORED_VALUE_NOT_FOUND` and are throttled per user. No public lookup or redeem endpoint exists.
- Seven new permissions: `commerce.stored_value_programs.{read,update}`, `commerce.stored_value.{read,create,update}`, `commerce.stored_value_adjustments.create` (separate from issuing), `commerce.stored_value_reconcile.approve`. Existing tenants do not gain them retroactively. Redeeming needs only the existing `commerce.pos.create` / `commerce.payments.create`.
- **Backward compatible, opt-in.** The whole surface is behind the new `storedValue` feature flag, which defaults OFF: with it off every owner route answers `409 FEATURE_DISABLED`, the sidebar entry is hidden and a card tender is refused before anything is written; existing POS/payment payloads, hashes and responses are unchanged (the payment record gains an additive `storedValue: null`).
- Heads-up for reviewers merging sibling branches: `sql/981` drops and re-adds `awcms_commerce_orders_payment_method_check` (adding `gift_card`, `store_credit` to the legacy summary hint), the same constraint any other branch that widens `payment_method` touches; and `commerce-features.ts` / `commerce-admin-labels.ts` / the permission-count test gain lines next to other branches' flags.
- Not here yet (ADR-0029 Deferred): a scheduled expiry job, a customer-facing balance view or any public redeem, refund to a lapsed card, selling a card as a catalogue product, store credit issued from a return.
