# POS operational reports: tenders, cash-up variance, expenses, loyalty and stored value (issue #296, ADR-0035)

The commerce reports screen could show sales by day, product and category, plus a live tender mix. A shop running a till also needs to know, per register and per day, what came in by which tender, who was short at close, what was spent, and how many loyalty points and how much gift-card money it still owes — and to trust those figures. Five new `reporting` projections (migrations `998`–`999` under `apps/cms/sql/`, starting at `apps/cms/sql/998_awcms_commerce_operational_reports_schema.sql`) answer that on the reporting engine that already exists: no second analytics engine. Decision: `docs/adr/0035-pos-operational-reports-are-commerce-projections-over-the-existing-ledgers-on-the-reporting-engine.md`.

- New: `commerce.pos_tender_daily` (payments and reversals per day, register and tender, by the day a leg settled), `commerce.pos_cash_up_variance` (expected, counted, corrections and variance per closed shift and tender), `commerce.pos_expense_daily` (posted and reversed per business day, category and tender; a reversal subtracts from the day it was posted on), `commerce.pos_loyalty_daily` (points earned, redeemed, expired, adjusted and reversed per day, with the outstanding points) and `commerce.pos_stored_value_daily` (gift-card and store-credit movement per day, with the outstanding liability per kind). Each is rebuildable from its ledger and a rebuild reproduces the live rows byte for byte; each reconciles by control totals recomputed through the same code; days are bucketed in `Asia/Jakarta`.
- New endpoints, `GET /api/v1/reports/commerce/operational-{tenders,cash-ups,expenses,loyalty,stored-value}` (`?from&to`, at most 366 days) and a `.csv` sibling each. CSVs are formula-neutralised, `no-store`, and audited as `operational_report.export` (family, range, row count — never a cell). `/admin/commerce-reports` gains five panels, each shown only to a holder of that report's permission.
- Ten new permissions, one `read` and one high-risk `export` per family: `commerce.report_tenders.*`, `commerce.report_cash_ups.*`, `commerce.report_expenses.*`, `commerce.report_loyalty.*`, `commerce.report_stored_value.*`. None is implied by `reporting.dashboard.read` or by a source-domain permission, and none opens a source row. Existing tenants do not gain them retroactively — grant them to the roles that should see each report.
- **Backward compatible, opt-in by feature.** A family whose source feature (`register`, `expenses`, `loyalty`, `storedValue`; all default OFF) is off answers `200` with `enabled: false` and no rows, and its CSV is a header row. The projections fill on the next `reporting:projections:refresh` (or an on-demand rebuild) — nothing is backfilled by the migration.
- Hardening: five new tables with FORCE RLS and `WITH CHECK`; four `security_invoker` source views and four partial cursor indexes; `awcms_worker` grants asserted in `security-readiness.ts`; retention (3650-day ceiling) and subject-data descriptors.
- Not here yet (ADR-0035 Deferred): stock balance / movement / low-stock, receiving, tax, margin, discount and bundle reports (their source capabilities are not built), and the returns slice (#287) — the ADR records the contract it follows.
