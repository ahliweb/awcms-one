# Commerce payments become an append-only allocation ledger (issue #285, ADR-0025)

An order's payment was one column (`payment_method`) plus a `payment_status` the status machine overwrote to `paid`. It is now a ledger — `awcms_commerce_payment_allocations` (migrations `940`–`943` under `apps/cms/sql/`, starting at `apps/cms/sql/940_awcms_commerce_payment_allocations_schema.sql`) — so one sale can be settled by several tenders, left partly due, and refunded by compensating rows, with settlement DERIVED from the rows and `payment_status` kept as a cache of it, independent of the order lifecycle. The "why" is in [ADR-0025](../docs/adr/0025-payments-are-an-allocation-ledger-separate-from-order-status.md).

- New: staff record a payment (`POST /api/v1/commerce/orders/{id}/payments`, `commerce.payments.create`) or a reversal (`.../payments/{paymentId}/reversals`, `commerce.payments.revoke`) under `Idempotency-Key`; `GET .../payments` and `GET /api/v1/reports/commerce/{tender-mix,outstanding-balances}` (`commerce.payments.read`); events `awcms.commerce.payment.{recorded,reversed}`.
- New: POS accepts an explicit `tenders[]` (split QRIS + cash + bank transfer; change computed from the cash leg only) and, with the separate permission `commerce.pos_due.create`, `allowDue` for an explicit balance due. The legacy single-tender `payment` payload keeps working unchanged. The order detail screen shows the ledger with record/reverse forms; the POS screen takes split tender lines and prints every tender; the reports screen shows tender mix and outstanding balances.
- **Behaviour changes to know about:** accepting a storefront manual-transfer confirmation now records a ledger leg instead of flipping the order — a confirmation for less than the total leaves the order `pending_payment` / `partially_paid` with the balance explicit (a down-payment order is still released on its down payment, now shown as `dp_paid`); a manual `PATCH .../status -> paid` on an order that has ledger legs but is not settled is refused with `409 PAYMENT_NOT_SETTLED` (record the payment instead), while on an order with no leg at all it records one full-amount manual leg (audited, idempotent) and releases the order, so COD / offline tenants keep working; a storefront gateway session is refused with `409 ORDER_PARTIALLY_SETTLED` for an order that already holds received money; the expiry job never expires (or restocks) such an order; a reused `Idempotency-Key` against another order or with a different payload is `409 IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` at the ledger level too; `orders.payment_status` gains `partially_paid`; the Midtrans webhook/reconcile paths and `createGatewaySession` now write the ledger (a pending leg at checkout, resolved idempotently by the verified outcome).
- Hardening: `awcms_app` loses `DELETE` on the ledger and a trigger freezes every column but the pending-gateway-leg resolution; composite tenant-safe FKs; every writer locks the order row (`FOR NO KEY UPDATE`).
- Migration: `sql/943` backfills one deterministic `backfill` leg per already-paid order (idempotent; a down-payment order's cached status is corrected to `dp_paid`).
- Not here yet (ADR-0025 Deferred): store-credit/gift-card tenders, automatic provider refunds, a gateway leg for less than the whole total.
