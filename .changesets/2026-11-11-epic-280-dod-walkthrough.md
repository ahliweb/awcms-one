# Epic #280: Definition of Done walkthrough

- `docs/aw-business-platform-dod.md` (with its Indonesian mirror) lists the verdicts, what each Partial item lacks, and who owns the remainder: this repository, upstream `awcms`, or the derived product that deploys (ADR-0024).
- The DoR tracker carries an "Archived" note pointing to it; its body is unchanged.
- The documentation index lists the new page beside the DoR tracker.
- No code, migration or setting changes.
