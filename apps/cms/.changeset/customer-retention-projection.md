feat(commerce): commerce-only customer-retention projection on the reporting engine (Issue #364, ADR-0044; spec `docs/aw-business-platform-metrics.md` section 6)

One new `commerce` `reportingProjection`, `commerce.customer_retention`, with no second analytics store (ADR-0040 D5.2) and no engine change. Retention is not additive - a late event moves a customer between cohorts - so the dimensional sink keeps ONE ROW PER CUSTOMER and RECOMPUTES it from the customer's current orders whenever one is paid, cancelled, returned or refunded, instead of applying a delta. Tables (`sql/1020`: FORCE RLS with `WITH CHECK`, `awcms_worker` grants mirrored in `security-readiness.ts`): `awcms_commerce_report_retention_customers` (`first_event_at`, the earliest qualifying instant strictly after it, `qualifying_order_count`, the `Asia/Jakarta` `cohort_month`; no name, contact or order id, no foreign key) and `awcms_commerce_report_retention_restated` (the 35-day restatement log, written only on the live path and left alone by a rebuild reset), plus a `security_invoker` view of settled payment-reversal legs as the second cursor stream (a hand-booked full refund moves `payment_status` with no order-status event). `sql/1021`: two permission rows. A rebuild reproduces the customer table byte for byte; reconcile compares three control totals recomputed through the same pure function.

Rules: a qualifying order is paid, in a paid state and not `payment_status = refunded` (a partial refund still qualifies); the repeat is a distinct order strictly after the first and within 90 x 24 h of it, the final instant inside, evaluated at read; the cohort is the `Asia/Jakarta` month of the first order; a cohort is "to date" until its windows have elapsed, shows counts and no percentage under 20 customers, and is flagged `restated` when a late event changes it after its 35-day window. Blocked and purged customers and the walk-in placeholder are outside every cohort (read-time join); the placeholder's orders are `unlinkedOrders`.

New endpoints (`reporting` work class): `GET /api/v1/reports/commerce/retention?months=` (`commerce.report_retention.read`; counts only) and `.csv` (`commerce.report_retention.export`, high-risk; aggregates, formula-neutralised, `no-store`, audited as `retention_report.export`). New per-tenant feature `retention`, default OFF: the routes answer `200` with `enabled: false`, the screen renders nothing and the CSV is a header row. `/admin/commerce-reports` gains a Customer retention panel (`CommerceRetentionReport.astro`: `.data-table-scroll`, named region, `data-label` on every cell, status pills). Two new permissions, existing `AccessAction` verbs only. Retention (cursor `cohort_month`, 3650 days) and subject-data descriptors in `domain/retention-lifecycle.ts`. No domain events and no consumers added.

Tests: `tests/commerce-retention-domain.test.ts` (pure rules, the section 6.3 example, the day-90 boundary, restatement, registration and permission separation), `tests/integration/commerce-retention.integration.test.ts` (rebuild equals live, reconcile, late events, Jakarta month boundary, small group, exclusions, restatement log, toggle, no second store, export, RLS) and `tests/integration/commerce-retention-routes.integration.test.ts` (permission matrix, CSV, audit, cross-tenant).
