feat(commerce): gift cards and store credit as a closed-loop liability ledger (Issue #288, epic #281)

New tables `awcms_commerce_stored_value_programs`, `awcms_commerce_stored_value_accounts`, `awcms_commerce_stored_value_ledger` (`sql/980` schema + triggers + REVOKEs, `sql/981` the payment-ledger integration, `sql/982` seven permissions, `sql/983` worker purge grants; `984` held). The ledger is append-only (`awcms_app` has no UPDATE/DELETE, a trigger refuses every UPDATE); the `BEFORE INSERT` trigger `awcms_commerce_stored_value_ledger_apply` is the only writer of an account's `balance`/`version`/`status` (it locks the account `FOR NO KEY UPDATE`, applies the kind/status/expiry rules, refuses a negative balance or one past the program ceiling and numbers the entry), an update guard refuses any other edit of those columns (except writing `balance`/`version` to exactly what the ledger sums to — what reconcile's repair does), and a deferred constraint trigger requires an `issue` entry for every account at COMMIT. The redeemable code is never stored: `code_hash` is `sha256:` over a tenant-scoped domain string and the normalised code, plus `code_last4`; the plaintext (100-bit CSPRNG, 32-symbol alphabet, check character) is returned once by the issuing request, whose idempotency record is saved with `code: null`.

Payment integration: `awcms_commerce_payment_allocations.tender_type` gains `gift_card`/`store_credit` and `stored_value_account_id` (composite FK, frozen by the replaced append-only guard, CHECKed to exist for exactly those tenders and never beside a `provider_reference`); the allocation and its mirror `redeem`/`refund` ledger entry are written together (a deferred constraint trigger refuses a leg without its mirror; the ledger trigger refuses an entry that does not match a succeeded leg of the same account, tender and amount); `awcms_commerce_orders.payment_method`'s CHECK is widened with the two values. `application/stored-value-ledger.ts` is the one writer; lock order is order row → account row; POS locks and checks its accounts (sorted by id) before the order row exists and throws a typed refusal before any write; `StoredValueInvariantError` (never mapped to a response) rolls a half-settled sale back.

New endpoints (all behind the `storedValue` feature flag, default OFF → `409 FEATURE_DISABLED`; every mutation but the program PUT and the sweep requires `Idempotency-Key`):

- `GET /api/v1/commerce/stored-value/programs`, `PUT .../programs/{kind}` (`commerce.stored_value_programs.{read,update}`)
- `GET`/`POST .../stored-value/accounts` (list / issue — `commerce.stored_value.{read,create}`), `GET .../accounts/{id}`, `GET .../accounts/{id}/ledger`
- `POST .../accounts/{id}/load` (`commerce.stored_value.create`), `/adjust` (`commerce.stored_value_adjustments.create`), `/status` (`commerce.stored_value.update`), `POST .../stored-value/expire` (`commerce.stored_value.update`), `POST .../stored-value/reconcile` (`commerce.stored_value.read`; `repair: true` also `commerce.stored_value_reconcile.approve`)
- `GET /api/v1/reports/commerce/stored-value`

`POST /api/v1/commerce/pos/orders` (`tenders[]`) and `POST /api/v1/commerce/orders/{id}/payments` accept `tenderType: gift_card | store_credit` with a `storedValueCode` (a tender without one hashes and replays exactly as before; with one, the idempotency request hash carries the tenant-scoped hash, never the plaintext); new errors `404 STORED_VALUE_NOT_FOUND`, `409 STORED_VALUE_UNAVAILABLE`, `409 STORED_VALUE_INSUFFICIENT`, `429 STORED_VALUE_LOOKUP_THROTTLED` (30 lookups/minute/user), and `PAYMENT_NOT_REVERSIBLE` reasons `stored_value_refund_not_allowed` / `stored_value_account_unavailable`. Event `awcms.commerce.stored_value.entry_recorded` on the account aggregate (registered in `module.ts`, the event-type registry and AsyncAPI). `dataLifecycle`/`subjectData` descriptors for all three tables (`domain/stored-value-lifecycle.ts`). Admin: `/admin/commerce-stored-value`, gift-card/store-credit tender lines on the POS screen and the order detail, a "Gift cards and store credit" toggle in the commerce Features section.
